# SERA — Recommendation Engine & Official CAPA / PAA Plans

---

## 1. Action Plan Architecture

SERA separates recommendations into two distinct industrial timelines:
1. **Immediate Corrective Actions (CAPA — Turnaround Scope)**: Emergency physical steps required to repair active defects, correct shaft misalignment, replace worn elastomer elements, and restore safe baseline within the turnaround window ($14.0\text{ hours}$).
2. **Preventive & Pro-Active Recurrence Controls (PAA — Fleet Strategy)**: Long-term engineering governance, scheduled alignment checklists, elastomer lifecycle management, and route interval adjustments to prevent recurrence plant-wide.

## 2. Official Action Plan for BL-5702 (AR-2026-OPP-0203)

Derived directly from official Abnormality Report **AR-2026-OPP-0203** (Slides 9 & 10):

### A. Immediate Corrective Actions (CAPA — Field Turnaround)
1. **Replace Worn Coupling Element**: Dismantle coupling guard, remove fatigued/cracked elastomer element, and fit genuine OEM replacement (PIC: ROT-01).
2. **Correct Soft-Foot Condition**: Measure motor drive-end feet; correct $0.12\text{ mm}$ gap using Grade 304 stainless steel precision shims until gap $< 0.05\text{ mm}$ (PIC: ROT-01).
3. **Precision Laser Shaft Realignment**: Align motor shaft to blower rotor shaft to $< 0.05\text{ mm}$ radial and angular tolerance using laser alignment tool (PIC: ROT-01).
4. **Restart & Load Verification**: Restart BL-5702 and confirm continuous stable operation at $38\text{ T/H}$ full powder handling section throughput.

### B. Preventive & Pro-Active Actions (PAA — Recurrence Prevention)
- **PM-1 (6-Monthly Alignment Inspection)**: Add mandatory laser alignment and soft-foot verification into BL-5702 routine PM schedule (PIC: ROT-01).
- **PM-2 (Coupling Element Life Register)**: Track cumulative operating hours of flexible spiders and mandate replacement at 12 months (PIC: REL-05).
- **PM-3 (Weekly Vibration Route)**: Shorten vibration monitoring route interval from monthly to weekly for critical Class-A train BL-5702 (PIC: REL-05).
- **PAA (Plant-Wide Fleet Rollout)**: Roll out alignment and soft-foot checklist to all Class-A rotating blowers across Orion Polypropylene Plant (OPP) by 18-Aug-2026.
