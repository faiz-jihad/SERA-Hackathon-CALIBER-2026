# SERA — Future Development Roadmap

---

## 🗺️ Release Roadmap (Phases 2 & 3)

### Phase 2: Industrial Connectivity & Enterprise Integrations (Q3 2026)
- **OPC-UA / MQTT IoT Streaming**: Native real-time streaming connectors to Yokogawa Centum VP, Emerson DeltaV, Honeywell Experion, and OSIsoft PI System historians.
- **Automated SAP S/4HANA RFC Connector**: Direct bidirectional integration with SAP PM module to automatically create Maintenance Notifications (`IW21`) and Work Orders (`IW31`).
- **WebGL 3D Waterfall FFT Spectrogram**: High-performance interactive 3D spectral waterfall plots in the browser.

### Phase 3: Advanced Predictive Intelligence & Edge Deployment (Q4 2026+)
- **Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs)**: Combining rotor-dynamics finite element models with operational telemetry for Remaining Useful Life (RUL) estimation.
- **Multimodal Oil & Thermography Ingestion**: Automated ingestion of FLIR infrared thermography images and spectrochemical oil analysis laboratory reports.
- **Edge Deployment (SERA Node)**: Containerized deployment on industrial edge gateways (e.g. Advantech, Siemens IPC) for zero-latency on-premise interlock monitoring.
